Serving the Community with Purpose

A clear identity, history and direction for a people-focused local administration.

Brief History

Egbe-Idimu Local Council Development Area is one of the councils created from the former Alimosho Local Government Area as part of Lagos State's effort to bring governance and development closer to communities.

The LCDA was established in 2003 and covers communities including Egbe, Idimu and Isheri-Olofin. The council's activities support grassroots administration, local infrastructure, community services and social development.
