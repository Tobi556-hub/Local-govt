About Egb-Idimu Local Government Council

Egb-Idimu Local Government Council is committed to providing effective public services and supporting sustainable development within the local community.

Through collaboration with residents, community organizations and relevant government institutions, the council seeks to improve infrastructure, public services and the general wellbeing of residents.
